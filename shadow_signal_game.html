The Quiet Grid

Move from ◆ to EXIT. Collect signals, avoid traps, and conserve energy.

Press “Start challenge” to begin. You can move one square up, down, left, or right.

How to win

◆
Plan your route

Tap a neighboring square. Diagonal moves are not allowed.

✦
Collect 3 signals

Each signal is worth 100 points. Reach EXIT for a 250-point bonus.

✕
Watch your energy

Every move costs 1 energy. Traps cost 2 extra energy and subtract 60 points.
